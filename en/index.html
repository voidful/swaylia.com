Auto-layout posts & stories

Swaylia turns your photos and words into beautiful posts — automatically. Pick a size, drop in a photo, a line of text or a quote, and Swaylia arranges the type, spacing and decoration around your main element. Not quite right? Swipe through more layouts or tap Shuffle.

  • Auto layout

    your main element always reads first; supporting elements move out of its way and never overlap

  • A million combinations

    12 style families and 67 design DNAs, every layout checked for legibility

  • Three social sizes

    4:5 post for Instagram and Threads, 9:16 story, 1:1 square

  • Fine-tune anything

    drag, resize, rotate, snap to guides, filters, fonts, shadows and motion

  • 811 searchable quotes — mostly Chinese, plus English and Japanese — that become your headline in one tap
  • Projects save automatically on your device — come back to edit, duplicate or rename
  • Available in 40+ languages, including English, 繁體中文, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch, العربية and हिन्दी
